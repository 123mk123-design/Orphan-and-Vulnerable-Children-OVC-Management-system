# Orphans & Vulnerable Children (OVC) Management System
## Comprehensive User Manual

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the Orphans & Vulnerable Children (OVC) Management System. This system is designed to streamline the process of identifying, verifying, and supporting vulnerable children through a coordinated effort between community members, local leaders, schools, and the Department of Social Welfare (DPMO).

The system consists of two primary platforms:
- **OVC Mobile Application**: Used by Guardians, Identifiers, Community Leaders, and School Heads.
- **OVC Web Dashboard**: Used by DPMO Administrators to manage the program at a national level.

---

## 2. Mobile Application Guide (For Applicants & Identifiers)

### 2.1 Mobile Application Overview & Welcome Screen
Launch the OVC Mobile App to access applicant services, view program information, or navigate to login/registration.

![Welcome & Authentication Screen](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101142.jpg)

### 2.2 User Registration
1. On the welcome screen, tap **Register** / **Create Account**.
2. Fill in your personal details (Full Name, Phone Number, Email, and Password).
3. Select your role as **Applicant** (if applying for a child in your care) or **Identifier** (if you are a community worker registering children).
4. Tap **Sign Up** to create your account.

![Registration Interface](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101152.jpg)

### 2.3 Submitting an Application / Registering a Child
1. Log in to your account.
2. On your Portal Dashboard, tap **Register Child** / **Start New Application**.
3. Enter required personal and guardian information (Child's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Guardian Name, and Guardian Phone Number).
4. Provide location information by selecting the Region, Chiefdom, and School.
5. Tap **Submit Application**. The application enters the system with status `Draft` / `Pending` for verification.

![Child Registration Form Step 1](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101213.jpg)
![Child Registration Form Step 2](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101225.jpg)
![Child Registration Form Step 3](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101232.jpg)
![Form Confirmation & Submission](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_101242.jpg)

### 2.4 Tracking Application Status & Profile Details
- View real-time status counters (Identified, Verified) and detailed timelines on your dashboard.
- Tap on any child record to view their full profile, current pipeline stage (`Draft` → `Community Verified` → `School Verified` → `DPMO Approved` → `Funded`), and documents.

## 3. Mobile Application Guide (For Community Leaders & Verifiers)

Community Leaders and School Heads use the mobile app to verify applications within their jurisdiction.

### 3.1 Community Leader Portal & Verification
1. Log in using your assigned leader credentials.
2. Review pending applications submitted within your Chiefdom.
3. Tap **Verify** to confirm community residency and approve the application for school verification.
4. Track verified applications and review totals on your dashboard chips.

![Community Leader Portal & Case Verification](./manual/Screenshot_20260805_103118.png)

## 4. Web Dashboard Guide (For DPMO Administrators)

The Web Dashboard is the central administrative hub for managing the entire OVC program nationwide.

### 4.1 Overview Dashboard & Key Metrics
- Access real-time analytics including Total OVCs Registered, Pending Reviews, Funds Disbursed, Active Schools, and Regional Summaries.

![DPMO National Overview Dashboard](./manual/Screenshot%202026-08-05%20102206.png)

### 4.2 Application Review & Decision Workflow
1. Navigate to **Applications**.
2. Select any application to open the **Application Review Modal**.
3. Inspect eligibility scores, risk factors, vulnerability details, and uploaded documents.
4. Click **Approve Application** to approve funding, or **Reject** to deny with feedback.
   - *Note: Approving or rejecting an application triggers an automated email notification to the stakeholders.*

![Application Review & Approval Modal](./manual/Screenshot%202026-08-05%20102247.png)

### 4.3 Schools & User Onboarding Management
- Manage registered schools, assign school heads, view contact credentials, and monitor school-level enrollment.

![School Heads & Credential Management](./manual/Screenshot%202026-08-05%20102402.png)

### 4.4 Automated SMS & Email Notification Controls
- Access notification logs and test notification triggers across application events (`Application Submitted`, `Application Approved`, `Application Rejected`, `Appointment Scheduled`).

![SMS & Email Notification Trigger Testing](./manual/Screenshot%202026-08-05%20102429.png)

## 5. Troubleshooting & Support

- **Forgot Password**: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to reset your credentials.
- **Missing Schools/Chiefdoms**: If your School or Chiefdom is missing from the dropdowns, contact your DPMO Administrator to update the system Reference Data.
- **Notifications**: Automated notifications are delivered via SMS and Email (Resend.com integration) upon application submission, approval, or rejection.
